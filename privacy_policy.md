# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: November 9, 2026*

This Privacy Policy explains how personal data is processed when you use the What's the Word? mobile app ("the App").

## Controller

The controller responsible for processing your personal data is:

**AlexJKL Oy** (Finland)
Email: [support@alexjkl.fi](mailto:support@alexjkl.fi)

## Data Stored on Your Device

The App does not require an account and stores your game progress, statistics and settings locally on your device. Game reminders, if you turn them on, are scheduled on your device and are not sent through our servers.

## Data Processed by Third-Party Services

The App uses the following services, which process data such as device identifiers (advertising ID, installation ID), IP address, device and OS information, and app or ad interactions:

- **Unity LevelPlay** (Unity Technologies) — ad mediation: selects which ad network serves each ad and measures ads. Collects advertising ID, IP address, device information, ad interactions (clicks, impressions, which ads you have seen) and SDK diagnostics. [Unity Privacy Policy](https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy)
- **Unity Ads** — serves ads through Unity LevelPlay. Collects advertising ID, device information, ad interactions and SDK diagnostics. [Unity Privacy Policy](https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy)
- **Liftoff Monetize (Vungle)** — serves ads through Unity LevelPlay. Collects advertising ID, device information, ad interactions and SDK diagnostics. [Liftoff Privacy Policy](https://liftoff.io/privacy-policy/)
- **Pangle** (Bytedance Pte. Ltd., Singapore) — serves ads through Unity LevelPlay. Collects advertising ID, IP address and approximate location derived from it, device information, ad interactions and SDK diagnostics. [Pangle Privacy Policy](https://www.pangleglobal.com/privacy/enduser-en)
- **Google User Messaging Platform** — shows the consent form where it is required and stores your choices on your device. To decide whether the form applies, Google processes your IP address and device information. [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- **Game Center (Apple) / Google Play Games** — leaderboards and achievements. If you are signed in, your player ID, player name, scores and achievements are processed by Apple or Google under their own terms. [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) · [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- **App Store / Google Play** — process the "Remove Ads" in-app purchase. We receive only a confirmation of the purchase, never your payment details.
- **Firebase (Google)**:
  - *Crashlytics* — crash and error reports: stack traces, OS version, device model, installation identifier.
  - *Analytics* — app usage events (for example, games started and completed, hints used, purchases) linked to an app instance identifier.
  - *Remote Config* — retrieves app configuration from Google's servers.

  [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

Ad networks act as independent controllers for the data they collect, or, where their terms provide so, as joint controllers with us (for example, Pangle under Art. 26 GDPR for certain processing). Their privacy policies linked above describe their processing in detail.

## Purposes and Legal Bases

| Purpose | Legal basis (GDPR) |
|---|---|
| Personalised ads | Your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) |
| Non-personalised ads and ad measurement | Your consent or our legitimate interest in funding the free version of the App (Art. 6(1)(a) or (f)), as shown in the consent form |
| "Remove Ads" purchase | Performance of a contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b)) |
| Leaderboards and achievements | Our legitimate interest in offering these game features (Art. 6(1)(f)) |
| Crash reporting and app configuration | Our legitimate interest in keeping the App stable and working correctly (Art. 6(1)(f)) |
| Usage analytics | Our legitimate interest in understanding and improving the App (Art. 6(1)(f)) |
| Answering your messages | Our legitimate interest in responding to you (Art. 6(1)(f)) |

## Your Consent Choices

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the App asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change or withdraw your consent at any time in the App under **Settings → Manage Consent**. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing carried out before the withdrawal.

On iOS, personalised ads also require your permission through App Tracking Transparency, which you can change in the iOS Settings app.

## International Transfers

Some of the services above process data outside the European Economic Area, in particular in the United States (Google, Apple, Unity, Liftoff) and in Singapore and the United States (Pangle), and their group companies and service providers may access data from other countries. Such transfers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where the recipient is certified under it, or on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

## Retention

- **Crash reports (Crashlytics)** — retained by Google for 90 days.
- **Analytics data** — retained according to Google's retention settings for Firebase Analytics.
- **Data collected by ad networks, Apple and Google (Game Center, Google Play Games, purchases)** — retained according to their own privacy policies.
- **Your messages to us** — kept as long as needed to handle your request, then deleted.

## Your Rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

- access your personal data and receive a copy of it
- have inaccurate data corrected
- have your data deleted
- restrict processing
- data portability
- object to processing based on legitimate interests
- withdraw your consent at any time

To exercise your rights, contact us at [support@alexjkl.fi](mailto:support@alexjkl.fi). Because most data is processed by the services listed above under device identifiers we cannot link to you, we may need to direct some requests to those services. You can also contact them directly through their privacy policies.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, in particular in the EU country where you live or work. In Finland, this is the [Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman](https://tietosuoja.fi/en).

## Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children below the age of digital consent in their country (13–16 in the EU). If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The current version is always available on this page, and the "Last updated" date shows when it was last changed.
